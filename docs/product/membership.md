# Membership, Usage, and Unlocks

## Objective

Offer useful free functionality while controlling variable AI and voice costs.

## Suggested tiers

### Free

- Core Project and Task workflow
- Limited Nova text usage
- Two starter companion choices

### Standard

- Increased Nova usage
- Expanded coaching
- More memory capacity
- Additional characters

### Premium / VIP

- Higher chat and voice limits
- More companion personalities
- Advanced memory and coaching
- Early access to new capabilities

## Achievement unlocks

Characters may also be unlocked through product progress:

- Completed tasks
- Completed projects
- Milestones
- Growth points

Subscription and achievement unlocks can coexist.

## Usage controls

- Track model and voice consumption
- Define monthly allowances
- Warn users before limits are reached
- Prevent unexpected charges
- Keep pricing and limits transparent

## Pricing note

Pricing discussed during planning is provisional and must be validated against model cost, app-store fees, taxes, support, and expected usage before publication.
